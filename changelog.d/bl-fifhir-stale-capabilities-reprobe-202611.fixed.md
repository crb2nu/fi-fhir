- IDE tabs no longer keep stale capabilities until they are reloaded. `accessCapabilities.ts` gains `refresh()`, which re-reads `/api/auth/status` with the current credential, and `refreshSoon()`, which runs at most one re-read per 2 s. The credential gate (`GraphQLCredentialGate.svelte`) calls `refresh()` once a bearer validates. `graphqlFetch` (`client.ts`) and `describeConnectionFailure` (`connectionsErrors.ts`, which also handles intake refusals) call `refreshSoon()` on a `legacy integration execution is unavailable` refusal. After a rollback of the session flip, intake, connections and Copilot re-derive from the new status. `docs/operations/INTEGRATION-SESSIONS.md` no longer tells operators to reload the tab.
