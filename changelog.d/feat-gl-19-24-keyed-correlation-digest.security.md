- Serve now renders the caller `X-Correlation-ID` on spans and log lines as a keyed `hmac-sha256:<32 hex>` digest under the new `FI_FHIR_TELEMETRY_CORRELATION_KEY` serve secret, instead of a dictionary-reversible unkeyed SHA-256. Unset keeps the `sha256:` digest with a startup WARN; a key under 32 bytes refuses to start (`cmd/fi-fhir/serve_tracing_correlation.go`, `cmd/fi-fhir/serve_tracing.go`, `cmd/fi-fhir/main.go`, `.env.example`, `docs/operations/PRODUCTION-HARDENING.md`, `docs/operations/PHI-RETENTION.md`).
