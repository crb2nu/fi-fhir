- `fi-fhir serve` can export OpenTelemetry spans over OTLP/HTTP. Tracing is opt-in and off by default: `FI_FHIR_TRACING_ENABLED` turns it on, `FI_FHIR_TRACING_ENDPOINT` names the HTTP(S) collector (required when enabled) and `FI_FHIR_TRACING_SAMPLER` sets the root sampling ratio (default `0.1`). Admission, workflow-plan and delivery operations become spans whose attributes are limited to `PermittedSpanAttributes()`, and operation logs carry the trace/span IDs (MR !279; `internal/observability/tracing.go`, `cmd/fi-fhir/serve_tracing.go`, `docs/user-guide/cli-reference.md`).
