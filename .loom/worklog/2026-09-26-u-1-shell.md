- Findings for other lanes:
  - The document region pads 12 px unless the route renders a `Toolbar`
    (`.ide-document:has(.ui-toolbar)` → 0): U-2/U-3 routes that adopt the
    page pattern own their edges.
  - `e2e/support.ts` `openIDE` now waits for `data-testid="access-chip"` to
    read "Trusted network"; the strip text lives in the chip's popover and in
    a hidden `role=status` announcement.
  - Local screenshots: two Vite dev servers sharing one `node_modules`
    (symlinked) clobber `node_modules/.vite` and serve 504 "Outdated Optimize
    Dep" → SvelteKit 500 page; run one at a time or restart with `--force`.
    Dev builds need `VITE_FI_FHIR_INTEGRATION_SESSION_ENABLED=true` and
    `VITE_FI_FHIR_PREVIEW_INTEGRATION_ID=adt-east` for e2e check 3.
  - Svelte 5 runes: `bind:this={map[key]}` into a plain object warns
    `binding_property_non_reactive` (U-0's `Tabs.svelte` does this); query the
    DOM from the event's `currentTarget` instead.
